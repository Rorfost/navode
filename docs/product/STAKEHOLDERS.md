# Stakeholders

- **Primary user and product owner:** defines daily workflow needs and accepts product trade-offs.
- **Rorfost:** product organization and repository owner.
- **Future contributors:** maintain the code, documentation, and quality standards.
- **Future users:** depend on a fast, private, understandable experience.
- **Integration providers:** may provide optional GitHub, Codeforces, calendar, or health data under their own policies.
- **Chrome Web Store and browser platform:** govern extension distribution and permissions.
